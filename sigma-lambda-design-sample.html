Chapter history

A hundred years of showing up for this city.

Since 1925, Sigma Lambda has stood alongside New Orleans through every era — brothers who led in courtrooms, city hall and the classroom, all while building the fraternity here at home. The redesigned site puts that history front and center, not buried in a submenu.

1925
AT

A.P. Tureaud

Civil rights attorney who argued for equal education in Louisiana courts for over three decades.

1946
DM

Ernest "Dutch" Morial

First Black mayor of New Orleans, and the first Black graduate of LSU Law School.

1962
EB

Emmett Bashful

Educator and administrator who helped shape higher education access across the city.

1974
CT

Charles Teamer, Sr.

Chancellor and civic leader whose work strengthened Black colleges in New Orleans.
